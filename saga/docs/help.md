# In-game help: draft text

The text for the help page the game will have (phase 3 of the [roadmap](roadmap.md)). It gives just enough to get the game rolling and leaves everything else to discovery or the [manual](manual.md).

---

## How to play

**Your habits are your companions.** Add a habit on the Roster and choose what it trains. If you're not sure which attribute fits, the manual has examples of each.

**Do it, then strike.** When you've done a habit in real life, go to battle and attack with its companion. Every blow is a real repetition, and only you know if it was honest.

**Tasks are spells.** Add one-off tasks to the Spellbook. When you finish one, a companion of its school casts it in battle.

**Log your vices.** When a bad habit happens, log it. It will cost you.

**Five fight at a time.** Choose the five you're working on now. The rest wait on the bench.

**Rest at the inn. Visit the shop.** Victories pay gold, so spend it.

The rest is for you to find out.

Want the answers anyway? [Read the manual.](manual.md)
