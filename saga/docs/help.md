# In-game help

The text of the help behind the **?** at the top of every screen (`helpHtml` in `web/views.js`). It gives just enough to get the game rolling and leaves everything else to discovery or the [manual](manual.md). Change the two together.

---

## How to play

**Your habits are your companions.** Call one from the Roster and choose what it trains.

**Do it, then strike.** When you've done a habit in real life, go to battle and attack with its companion. Every blow is a real repetition, and only you know if it was honest.

**Tasks are spells.** Write one-off tasks in the Spellbook. When you finish one, a companion of its school casts it in battle.

**Pay your tithes.** Name your vices on the Roster. When one gets the better of you, pay the tithe. It will cost you.

**Five walk with you.** Choose the five you're working on now. The rest wait on the bench.

**Rest at the inn. Visit the shop.** Victories pay gold, so spend it.

### What a habit can train

- 🏃 **BODY** What moves you: walking, Pilates, any exercise.
- 🔥 **VITALITY** What keeps the body going: sleep, meals, water, rest.
- 📚 **MIND** What adds to what you know: books, courses, study.
- 🎸 **CRAFT** What you make: music, art, writing.
- 🕯️ **SPIRIT** What deepens your practice: meditation, prayer, reflection.
- 🧹 **ORDER** What keeps the house and your affairs in hand.
- ⚓ **RESOLVE** What goes past your limit: exceptional work, the extra effort, the thing you dodged.
- 🎭 **CHARISMA** What passes between people: calls, gatherings, sharing your work.

The rest is for you to find out.
