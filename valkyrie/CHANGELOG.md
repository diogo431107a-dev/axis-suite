## 0.0.4 — 2026-10-13

The first numbered version: Valkyrie as it runs in production on this date. Its 4 commits so far, this one included, give the number.

- A kanban board app: any number of boards per user, with lists of cards side by side.
- Cards have labels, a due date and a checklist. They move with the mouse, a finger or the keyboard (Ctrl+Shift+arrows, or Space to pick up).
