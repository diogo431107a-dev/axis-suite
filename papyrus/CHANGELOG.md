## 0.0.5 — 2026-10-13

The first numbered version: Papyrus as it runs in production on this date. Its 5 commits so far, this one included, give the number.

- A journal with one page per day, in as many journals as you like. Each page can have a mood and `#tags`. Days that haven't come yet can't be written.
- Saves itself as you type, and keeps the text in the browser until the server has it.
- Writing prompts, templates, and an "On this day" view of earlier years.
- Journals go to a recycle bin for 30 days.
