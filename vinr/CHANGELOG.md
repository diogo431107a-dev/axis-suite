## 0.0.6 — 2026-10-25

- Clicking Vinr at the top left opens an About box with Vinr's version and the Axis Suite version it was built with. Every module of the suite opens its About box the same way.

## 0.0.4 — 2026-10-13

The first numbered version: Vinr as it runs in production on this date. Its 4 commits so far, this one included, give the number.

- A contacts manager. Each contact can have phones (marked for WhatsApp), emails, addresses, websites, a birthday, other dates and a memo.
- Contacts can be starred and put in groups, and go to a recycle bin for 30 days.
- Import and export as vCard and JSON.
